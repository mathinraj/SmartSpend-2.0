# I Got Tired of Finance Apps That Wanted My Data More Than They Wanted to Help Me

*So I built one that doesn't need your email, your bank login, or your trust.*

---

Last year, I opened a popular expense tracking app and it asked me to link my bank account. Again.

I'd already given it my email. My phone number. My name. And now it wanted direct access to my bank? To "make things easier"?

I closed the app and never opened it again.

That night, I started building **SpendTrak**.

---

## The Problem Nobody Talks About

Here's what most finance apps won't tell you: **they make more money from your data than from your subscription.**

You tell a finance app how much you earn, where you spend, what subscriptions you pay for, and how much debt you carry. That's not financial data — that's a complete behavioral profile.

And what do you get in return? A pie chart.

**You shouldn't have to trade your financial privacy for a basic tool that adds up your expenses.**

---

## So I Built SpendTrak

**SpendTrak** is a personal finance tracker that runs entirely in your browser. No sign-up. No servers. No data collection. No subscription.

You open it, you start tracking. Everything is stored locally on your device. Your data literally never leaves your browser unless *you* choose to back it up.

Here's what it does.

---

## Your Money, One Dashboard

Total balance, this month's income and expenses, recent transactions, all your accounts — at a glance.

No clutter. No ads. No "upgrade to premium to see your own data." Customize what shows up to match how *you* think about your money.

---

## All Your Accounts in One Place

Banks, credit cards, cash, UPI wallets, Bitcoin, investments — SpendTrak handles them all. Each account tracks its own balance. Transfers between accounts update both balances automatically.

You can even assign **real bank and app logos** to your accounts. Your HDFC account shows the HDFC logo. Your Paytm wallet shows the Paytm icon. Small touch, big difference.

---

## Split Bills Without the Awkwardness

You paid for dinner. Three friends owe you. Two weeks later, nobody remembers the amount, and you feel weird asking.

SpendTrak's built-in split tracker keeps a running balance of who owes whom. When someone pays you back, record the settlement — your bank balance updates automatically. No separate app needed.

---

## Analytics That Answer Real Questions

- **Where is my money going?** — Category breakdowns with charts
- **Am I spending more than last month?** — Side-by-side month comparison
- **How much am I saving?** — Savings rate, calculated automatically
- **What are my biggest expenses?** — Top 5, at a glance
- **How's my credit card?** — Utilization bars and spending per card

Not just charts. Answers.

---

## Never Miss a Bill

Track subscriptions, EMIs, rent, and recurring payments. SpendTrak shows overdue items on your dashboard, highlights what's due today, and sends browser notifications so you don't forget.

The "gentle nagging" your future self will thank you for.

---

## Sync Across Devices — On Your Terms

SpendTrak offers **optional** Google Drive sync. Your data goes to a private folder in *your own* Google Drive — not my servers. Push when you want, pull on another device when you're ready.

Don't want to connect Google? The app works perfectly without it.

You can also export everything as JSON, CSV, PDF reports, or Excel workbooks with charts. Your data, your format, anytime.

---

## Privacy Isn't a Feature — It's the Foundation

This is why SpendTrak exists:

- **No sign-up.** No email, no phone number required.
- **No servers.** I literally cannot see your data.
- **App lock.** PIN or password, with configurable timeout.
- **Hide balances.** One tap to mask all amounts on screen — perfect for checking in public.
- **Works offline.** No internet required. Ever.

In a world where every app wants to know everything about you, SpendTrak is deliberately ignorant of who you are. By design.

---

## Install It Like a Real App

Add SpendTrak to your home screen and it runs like a native app — fullscreen, fast, always ready. No app store, no 200MB download, no permissions maze.

---

## The One Catch

Since your data lives in the browser, clearing site data will clear SpendTrak too. That's the trade-off for zero servers.

That's why the app nudges you every 7 days to back up, and why Google Drive sync and exports exist. Use them.
